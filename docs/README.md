About NomadStay
NomadStay is a booking platform concept designed for digital nomads and remote workers.

The platform focuses on work-friendly accommodations by providing information such as:

Verified WiFi speed
Dedicated workspaces
Coworking availability
Long-stay options
Reviews from remote workers
Business travel support
Originally developed as a Scrum team project, NomadStay is now being further developed and maintained as an individual portfolio project.
